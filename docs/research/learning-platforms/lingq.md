# LingQ: Audit

> **Category:** General reading-and-listening platform with any-content import
> **Researched:** 2026-10-02 from lingq.com and the LingQ blog

## Snapshot

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Languages | 52+ with one membership | [1] |
| Library | 20,000+ news lessons, 10,000+ entertainment, 10,000+ culture, 4,000+ each in several topics | [1] |
| Free limits | "20 saved words or phrases as LingQs, total"; "5 custom lessons" imported | [2] |
| Premium | USD 14.99/month monthly; about USD 10/month on 12 months; USD 8.99/month on 24 months | [2] |

## Feature assortment

- **Import anything.** Browser extensions for YouTube, Netflix, TikTok, Spotify, Disney+, Prime, books, podcasts and articles, with AI-generated transcripts, translations and audio [1].
- **Highlighting of unknown words.** Saving one makes it a "LingQ", which then enters SRS review [1].
- **Known-word counts and statistics,** streaks and challenges [1].
- **Premium adds** full-sentence translations, auto-generated audio and offline access [2].

## Strengths

- **The known-word count is the progress metric.** It is honest and motivating: it measures reading ability, not points.
- **Every text is a lesson,** so learners never run out of material.
- **Reading and listening together** (generated audio).

## Weaknesses

- **The free tier is a demo.** Twenty saved words in total is too few to learn anything [2]. The blog itself presents free as a trial.
- **Import quality varies.** Machine transcripts and translations can mislead.
- **Thin on structure.** Beginners lack a guided path (analysis).

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **Japanese VMA articles are already lessons,** with words extracted on the backend. A known-word count and per-article "new words" count are mostly a query over existing data.
- **Do not cap the free tier so tightly it becomes a demo.** For Japanese VMA's membership spec, free lists and reading should stay useful.
- **The NHK import (#404) is Japanese VMA's version of the news library.** Each imported article automatically gets coverage stats.

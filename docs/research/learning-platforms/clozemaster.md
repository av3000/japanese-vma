## Feature assortment

- **Fill-in-the-blank exercises** using real sentences [1].
- **Multiple choice, or text input** "for an extra challenge" [1].
- **SRS:** "sentences come back for review at the right time" [1].
- **Progress tracked by word frequency,** with points and sentence mastery [1].

## Strengths

- **A dead-simple loop:** one sentence, one blank.
- **Frequency order** means the first thousand words learned are the thousand most useful.

## Weaknesses

- **Sentence quality varies** if the source corpus is crowd-sourced.
- **No teaching.** It is practice only.

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **A sentence cloze mode** can be built from Japanese VMA's stored Tatoeba sentences and the word–sentence links. It is listed in the flashcards epic's Phase 2 candidates.
- **Track progress by frequency band** ("top 1,000 words: 64% known") using the existing word `frequency` column.

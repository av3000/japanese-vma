# Duolingo: Audit

> **Category:** General gamified course app with a Japanese course
> **Researched:** 2026-10-02 from the Duolingo blog

## Feature assortment

- **Short lessons on a path,** with XP and weekly leagues [4].
- **A streak** of consecutive study days, protected by Streak Freeze [1].
- **Character tabs.** A kanji becomes practisable once it has appeared in a course word, and tapping it shows "the words you've already learned that use that character" [3].
- **Explain My Answer:** personalised feedback on the specific mistake made [2].

## Strengths

- **The habit loop is measured, not guessed.** Duolingo publishes the effect sizes [1].
- **Forgiveness raises persistence.** Streak Freeze increased daily active learners [1]. Flexibility beats strict rules.
- **Kanji are tied to known words.** A character unlocks only after the learner has met it in a word [3], so it is never studied in a vacuum.
- **Feedback at the moment of error** [2].

## Weaknesses

- **Shallow Japanese depth.** Gamified sentence-building does not reach intermediate reading (analysis; widely discussed by learners).
- **The gamification can be the goal.** Protecting a streak can beat actually learning (analysis).

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **Add a forgiving streak, not a strict one.** Count a day when any study happens (a flashcard session, finishing an article), and allow a freeze. The data says flexibility helps [1].
- **Copy the "words you know with this kanji" panel.** Japanese VMA's kanji detail can list words from the learner's catalogues or Known words that contain the kanji, rather than only all dictionary words.
- **Explain My Answer, Japanese VMA style.** After a wrong flashcard answer, an optional "Why?" calls the chat assistant with the item and the given answer.

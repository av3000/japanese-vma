# Busuu: Audit

> **Category:** General structured course (CEFR A1–C1) with community corrections
> **Researched:** 2026-10-02 from busuu.com; prices and certificate details from third-party reviews

## Snapshot

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Levels | "from A1 to C1", 2,500+ hours | [1] |
| Languages | 14+, including Japanese | [1] |
| Community | "Get feedback from fluent speakers in the Busuu Community"; 105+ million community interactions in 2024 | [1] |
| Price (third-party) | Premium €9.99/month monthly, about €5.83/month yearly; Premium Plus slightly higher | [2] |
| Certificates (third-party) | McGraw Hill co-branded certificates after a level test (A1–B2) | [2] |

## Feature assortment

- **Expert-designed lessons:** speaking, pronunciation, conversation, grammar, vocabulary, culture [1].
- **Community feedback:** written (and spoken) exercises are corrected by fluent speakers, who are themselves learners of the learner's native language [1], [2].
- **Study tracking** and a structured curriculum [1].

## Strengths

- **Human feedback on output.** Writing and speaking are where apps are weakest; real corrections fill that gap.
- **The exchange runs itself.** Learners of Japanese correct English, and Japanese speakers correct Japanese, so the community supplies its own reviewers.
- **Certificates** give a tangible milestone (per the third-party sources).

## Weaknesses

- **Correction quality varies** with whoever answers.
- **The Japanese course is shallower** than dedicated Japanese tools (an analysis judgement).
- **Prices and certificate terms** are not clearly published on the first-party pages read.

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **Japanese VMA already has community posts and comments.** A "Correct my Japanese" post type, where a learner writes a few sentences and others reply with a corrected version, reuses that engagement module. The gap is a correction display (an original vs corrected diff) and a "helpful" signal.
- **Moderation matters.** Human feedback needs reporting and moderation, which the admin role work already touches.
- **The AI version is the planned chat assistant** (Kanshudo and Duolingo already ship it). Human and AI corrections can sit side by side, clearly labelled.

# Renshuu: Audit

> **Category:** Japanese all-in-one (vocabulary, kanji, grammar, sentences) around a personal dictionary
> **Researched:** 2026-10-02 from renshuu.org and its official forums

## Snapshot

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Model | "Everything is built around one central dictionary that tracks the individual's knowledge" | [1] |
| Price | Free to start; renshuu Pro is offered monthly, as one-time year purchases and lifetime. Exact prices are not on the pages read | [1], [2] |
| Platforms | Web, iOS, Android | [1] |

## Feature assortment

- **Dictionaries** for vocabulary (JLPT N5–N1 and word-type filters), kanji (by grade and JLPT), grammar and sentences (audio-first) [1].
- **Quiz types:** multiple choice, writing, drag-and-drop, listening [1].
- **Furigana that adapts.** It is adjusted automatically based on the kanji the learner knows [1].
- **Gamification.** The mascot "Kao-chan" fights monsters as proficiency grows, a "Counter Punch" game teaches counters, and all of it can be customised or switched off [1].
- **Lesson packs** for textbooks (Genki, Tobira, Minna no Nihongo), JLPT levels and pop culture (Pokémon, Sailor Moon, Dragon Quest and others) [1].
- **User-created lessons** and shared study lists [1].

## Strengths

- **One knowledge model.** Because every feature reads the same "what you know" record, the app can personalise everywhere. Furigana is the visible example.
- **Gamification you can turn off.** It serves people who like it without patronising people who do not.
- **Breadth for free.** Vocabulary, kanji, grammar and sentences all live in one account.

## Weaknesses

- **Feature density.** So many modes and settings can overwhelm newcomers (analysis).
- **Pricing is not transparent** on the public pages read. Learners have to dig through forums.
- **Pop-culture packs** imply curated lists tied to copyrighted works. That is fine for vocabulary lists, but it is a curation burden.

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **Make "Known" a first-class signal, not just a catalogue.** Japanese VMA's `KNOWN_KANJIS`, `KNOWN_WORDS`, `KNOWN_RADICALS` and `KNOWN_SENTENCES` catalogue types already exist. Treating their union as the learner's knowledge model unlocks adaptive furigana, article coverage, and "new words only" filters.
- **Adaptive furigana in the article reader** is the single most visible personalisation (Satori Reader does the same).
- **Gamification must be optional.** If Japanese VMA adds streaks or badges, put an off switch in settings from day one.
- **Curated starter packs** (JLPT N5 kanji, N5 words) can be ordinary public catalogues owned by a system user, the same pattern as the planned NHK import's system user (#405).

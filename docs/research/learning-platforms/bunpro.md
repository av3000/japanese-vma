> **Researched:** 2026-10-02 from bunpro.jp and the official Bunpro community FAQ

## Feature assortment

- **Typed answers, not recognition.** "Since Bunpro requires that you manually input each answer, your ability to reproduce grammar drastically improves." [2]
- **Hints.** Learners can cycle through English translation levels, and near-miss answers are recognised as close [2].
- **Ghost reviews.** Missed sentences "come back to haunt you" in an extra 4 h / 12 h / 24 h / 48 h cycle alongside normal progression [2].
- **Textbook cross-references.** Each grammar point lists free external resources and page numbers in Genki, みんなの日本語 and Tobira, plus related-grammar comparisons for nuance [2].
- **Paths** for studying grammar page-by-page alongside a textbook [2].
- **Decks, graded reading passages, a kana trainer, content search and official forums** [1], [3].

## Strengths

- **Production over recognition.** Typing the form trains recall, which is the harder skill.
- **Leech handling without punishment.** Ghost reviews add practice for weak items without resetting their main progress.
- **It meets learners where they are.** Textbook paths and page numbers make Bunpro a companion to a course they already use, not a competitor to it.
- **A generous free tier.** The whole reference library and the reading passages are free [1]. Only scheduling is paid.

## Weaknesses

- **Fill-in-the-blank can be gamed.** Learners pattern-match the blank instead of reading the sentence (an analysis judgement, common with cloze).
- **Nuance between similar grammar** needs explanation that a single typed answer cannot assess.
- **Grammar lives apart from the learner's own reading.** Sentences are Bunpro's, not the articles the learner reads.

## Lessons for Japanese VMA

- **Leech handling for flashcards Phase 2.** Mark an item as a "leech" after N misses and give it an extra short cycle, rather than only resetting it.
- **Reference-first, schedule-paid** is a pricing shape worth copying in the membership spec. The dictionary and reading stay free; personal scheduling and stats are the premium layer.
- **Cross-reference textbooks** by tagging catalogues with chapter references (for example "Genki I, ch. 3") without reproducing the textbook's lists. Copying a publisher's word list verbatim is a licensing risk.
- **Hint ladders in typed modes.** The flashcards branch already has typed input, so a progressive hint (first kana, then meaning) is a small addition.
